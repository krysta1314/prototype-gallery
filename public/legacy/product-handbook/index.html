Introduction · from the product team, for sales reference

Chapter 05 · Enterprise

What Enterprise adds over individual logins

Enterprise is designed for organisations that need control over AI content: shared credits, admin oversight, brand safeguards and one contract instead of personal accounts.

Several individual Ultra logins

  • Credits locked to each person
  • No admin view of who made what
  • No approval step before an ad ships
  • Brand rules live in people's heads
  • Charged to personal cards

BuzzVideo Enterprise

  • One monthly credit pool for the whole organisation
  • Admin sets a limit per person; usage alerts at 50%, 80%, 100%
  • Approval steps, Brand Kit and Character Lock
  • Free, unlimited reviewer seats for brand and finance
  • API access; SSO from E2; SLA, IP indemnity and security review on E3
  • One annual contract and invoice
Chapter 06 · Enterprise pricing

Enterprise tiers, pricing rules and add-ons

Annual contracts priced by the Token PRD: 3× model cost, with month 1 at 2×. Tiers, pricing policies, credits by month, add-ons, and what each plan makes.

Tiers and prices

Annual contract · one credit pool for the organisation

Pricing policies

Fixed tier and seat prices

Tier and seat prices are fixed. Approved exceptions: one month's rollover of unused credits (up to 20% of the pool), or 1–2 bonus seats.

How credits work

Credits arrive monthly on both billing cycles and reset each cycle; unused credits expire. Free gets 500 credits once, no card needed.

Token pricing rules

From the Token PRD (pricing engine) · the pricing rules behind every plan

Customer typeStandard markupPrice ÷ model costFirst monthGross margin
Freemium / self-serve100%2×—50.0%
Enterprise — default200%3×100% (2×)66.7%
Enterprise — negotiated (example)300% (variable)4×200% (3×)75.0%

Price = real model cost × (1 + markup). Gross margin = markup ÷ (1 + markup) — markup is not margin. The first-month rate switches to the standard rate automatically on day 1 of month 2. Credits each model uses stay the same for every customer; the markup changes the price per credit.

Add-ons

Extra seats and extra credits on top of the tier · set in Admin when the contract is opened, or added later

Extra seats

TierPer seat / monthCan add

Each extra seat adds 2,000 credits a month to the shared pool. When E1 or E2 reach their cap, the next step is the tier above.

Extra monthly credits

$10 per 1,000 credits / month

Any amount, up to 10,000,000 credits a month, added to the pool on top of the tier's monthly credits. Set when the contract is opened; a higher monthly amount later means a renewal.

Adding during the contract

Add seats — takes effect immediately and is charged for the months left, e.g. 1 E3 seat with 11 months left = $39 × 11 = $429. Its 2,000 credits arrive with the next monthly release.

Add credits — a one-time purchase, priced off the tier (on E3, 100 credits = $1.00). Lands in the pool right away and is permanent: it stays until spent, across monthly resets and renewals.

Changing the tier or term means a new contract (renewal).

Chapter 08 · Product FAQ

Common questions about BuzzVideo

Answers come from the website FAQ, the Terms of Service and the product team.

Can customers use the ads commercially?

Yes, on every paid plan: ads, social, product pages and paid client work, including white-label agency deliverables. No royalties or per-use fees.

Website FAQ · full terms in the Terms of Service

Are customer prompts or images used to train AI?

No. Prompts, uploads and outputs stay private to the account, aren't used for training and aren't shared with third parties.

Website FAQ.

How does BuzzVideo compare on price with Creatify or HeyGen?

Entry prices are similar. The difference is scope: the Marketing Agent plans the campaign, every leading model is in one account, and teams get approvals and brand controls. Chapter 03 has the details.

Why does Enterprise cost more per credit than Ultra?

About $7.50 vs $5.00 per 1,000 credits (Enterprise is priced at 3× model cost, self-serve at 2×). The difference pays for API, approvals, Brand Kit, onboarding, support and security — and Enterprise still gives about 1.7× the credits of the same number of Ultra logins.

Can a team share one Ultra login?

No. Starter, Pro and Ultra are for one person each. Teams need Team, Scale or Enterprise, which add seats, shared workspaces and admin controls.

Website FAQ

Can customers pay monthly or cancel?

Self-serve plans: monthly or yearly, cancel anytime, access continues to the end of the period. No routine refunds. Enterprise is an annual contract.

Website FAQ

What happens when credits run out?

Add credits (they never expire), add seats (+2,000 credits each on Enterprise) or move up a tier. Admins get alerts at 50%, 80% and 100% of the pool.

Are discounts available?

Tier and seat prices are fixed. Approved exceptions are one month's rollover of unused credits (up to 20% of the pool) or 1–2 bonus seats.

Is there an API?

Yes, on Enterprise: Shopify product pages, internal creative pipelines, agency client portals and marketing tools like HubSpot or Braze. BuzzVideo MCP works inside Claude and ChatGPT on paid plans.

Website FAQ

Which models suit which work?

Standard models for everyday product shots and test variants; Premium Cinematic models for hero ads and brand campaigns. Most teams mix both.

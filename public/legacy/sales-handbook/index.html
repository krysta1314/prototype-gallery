Discovery questions

  1. How many ads or variants do you ship a month, and on which platforms?
  2. Who makes them today — in-house, an agency, freelancers? How long does one take?
  3. Which AI tools are people already paying for, and on whose card?
  4. How many people would create ads, and how many only review or approve?
  5. Does anything need approval or brand checks before it goes live?
  6. Does IT need SSO, a security review or a DPA? Does legal need IP indemnity?
  7. Do you want generation inside your own systems (API) or in Claude / ChatGPT (MCP)?
  8. Can you sign an annual contract, and who signs?

Chapter 05 · Why companies buy Enterprise

Why companies buy Enterprise instead of a few Ultra logins

Enterprise buyers pay to remove shadow accounts: personal logins on personal cards, off-brand and invisible to the admin.

"Every Ultra account outside your workspace is content your brand team never sees before it ships."

Buying several Ultra logins

  • Credits locked to each person
  • No admin view of who made what
  • No approval step before an ad ships
  • Brand rules live in people's heads
  • Charged to personal cards

BuzzVideo Enterprise

  • One monthly credit pool for the whole organisation
  • Admin sets a limit per person; usage alerts at 50%, 80%, 100%
  • Approval steps, Brand Kit and Character Lock
  • Free, unlimited reviewer seats for brand and finance
  • API access; SSO from E2; SLA, IP indemnity and security review on E3
  • One annual contract and invoice

The comparison procurement will make

Monthly credits for the same number of creators, billed annually.

Chapter 07 · Handling objections

What clients ask, and what to say

Answers follow the website FAQ and Terms where they exist. Anything marked "confirm" needs Ryan's OK before you promise it.

Can we use the ads commercially?

Yes, on every paid plan: ads, social, product pages and paid client work, including white-label agency deliverables. No royalties or per-use fees.

Website FAQ · full terms in the Terms of Service

Will our prompts or product images train your AI?

No. Prompts, uploads and outputs stay private to the account, aren't used for training and aren't shared with third parties.

Website FAQ. It also offers Enterprise data residency, SOC 2 reports and a custom DPA — confirm before promising; v1.6 lists the DPA on E3 only.

Why pay more than Creatify or HeyGen?

Compare the whole workflow: the Marketing Agent plans the campaign, every leading model is in one account, and teams get approvals and brand controls. For teams, compare against the cost of separate logins.

Enterprise costs more per credit than Ultra.

It does: about $9.46 vs $7.08 per 1,000 credits. The difference pays for API, approvals, Brand Kit, onboarding, support and security — and Enterprise still gives about twice the credits of the same number of Ultra logins.

Can we just share one Ultra login?

No. Starter, Pro and Ultra are for one person each. Teams need Team, Scale or Enterprise, which add seats, shared workspaces and admin controls.

Website FAQ

Can we pay monthly or cancel?

Self-serve plans: monthly or yearly, cancel anytime, access continues to the end of the period. No routine refunds; special cases are reviewed via info@buzzvideo.ai. Enterprise is an annual contract.

Website FAQ

What if we run out of credits?

Buy a top-up pack (valid 12 months), add seats (+2,000 credits each on Enterprise) or move up a tier. Admins get alerts at 50%, 80% and 100% of the pool.

Can you give us a discount?

Tier and seat prices don't move. Offer one month's rollover of unused credits (up to 20% of the pool) or 1–2 free seats. Anything else goes to Ryan / finance.

Do you have an API?

Yes, on Enterprise: Shopify product pages, internal creative pipelines, agency client portals and marketing tools like HubSpot or Braze. BuzzVideo MCP works inside Claude and ChatGPT on paid plans.

Website FAQ · MCP on Free: the website says yes, the product team says no — confirm

Which models should we use?

Standard models for everyday product shots and test variants; Premium Cinematic models for hero ads and brand campaigns. Most teams mix both.

Chapter 08 · Sales process and who to ask

From first call to signed contract

  1. QualifyAsk the discovery questions and pick a plan with the map in chapter 02.
  2. DemoRun it live on app.buzzvideo.ai with the client's own product. Never demo on UAT.
  3. TrialSelf-serve: the Free plan gives 500 credits with no card. Enterprise pilot: ask Ryan for a trial organisation To confirm
  4. ProposalQuote the tier from the rate card, show the "N Ultra logins" comparison, add seats or top-ups if needed.
  5. ApprovalAnything below list price, a first-month discount longer than one month, or custom credits needs Ryan / finance.
  6. Contract and setupAnnual contract. The organisation is created in BuzzVideo Admin with its tier, seats and credit pool, then onboarding. Seats can be added anytime; the difference is billed quarterly.
  7. GrowWatch usage alerts. A client that uses its pool by day 20 every month is ready to upgrade.
